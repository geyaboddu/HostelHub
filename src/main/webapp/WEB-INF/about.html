About HostelHub

HostelHub is a simple and convenient hostel room allocation system designed to make the room allocation process easier for students and hostel administrators.

Students can view available rooms, check room occupancy, and submit their preferred room allocation requests.

Hostel administrators can manage room allocation requests and efficiently handle hostel room availability.
